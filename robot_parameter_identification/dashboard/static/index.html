    Planner envelope

    Where the planner may take each joint. Left unset it uses the arm's own range, which describes the arm and not the cell it stands in. A cell is rarely symmetric: an arm mounted at an angle meets the bench swinging one way and nothing the other. Changing this clears any armed run, because the poses that were rehearsed are not the poses this will design.

    Gravity compensation

    Gravity identification

    Every pose is crossed both ways at two speeds. Standing still leaves static friction free to take any value in its band, so the pair mean is gravity and the half difference is friction. No acceleration here, so this model holds the arm up; it does not move it fast.

    Full campaign

    Optimal excitation identification

    Load-conditioned low-speed subtrajectories identify steady friction; distinct, cumulatively optimized multi-joint Fourier trajectories identify rigid dynamics. Separate Fourier trajectories remain untouched for validation.

    Load sweep

    Drives a speed ladder at a series of gravity loads, one joint at a time. The levels are searched for, not set: how hard gravity can load a joint depends on where every other joint stands, and on how far its own axis is from vertical. Joints it cannot load are swept once and said so.
